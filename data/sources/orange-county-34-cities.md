## Notes and limits

- Confirmed fact: Orange County contains 34 incorporated cities, as distinct from unincorporated county areas.
- Fast Track implication: potential geographic expansion across 34 city jurisdictions. This count alone does NOT establish that Fast Track supports 34 cities, that all cities have identical ADU rules, or that the county itself issues every city's permits.
- The homepage is a live page without a clearly stated publication date; the check date is recorded above.
