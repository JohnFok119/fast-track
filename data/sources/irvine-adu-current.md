## Official submittal requirements (paraphrased inventory; verify exact wording against source)

Source section: "Submittal Requirements" (web page; no page numbers).

1. ADU Verification Form, requested from the Planning Team.
2. Completed ADU permit application.
3. Building plans, calculations, and construction documentation.
4. Development Standards Checklist for Site Plan.
5. Hold Harmless Affidavit.
6. Covenant obtained from Planning, notarized and recorded with the Orange County Recorder when the planner instructs.
7. Affidavit signed by owner and applicant; electronic signature disclosure if digitally signed.
8. Developer Deposit Case Set-Up form.
9. School Fee Determination Form, included in application package.
10. Transportation Corridor Agency Form, included in application package.
11. Address Application, included in application package.

## Application workflow

Source section: "Submittal Instructions" and "Next Steps".

- Submit through IrvineReady! Online Permit Submittal Portal.
- Create an account or log in; begin a new submittal using the plus button.
- Under Project Type, choose "For Permitting--Other".
- City says it reviews submission completeness in approximately two business days.
- The city emails a fee payment link; review processing follows receipt of payment.
