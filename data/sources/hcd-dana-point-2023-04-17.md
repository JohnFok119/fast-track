## Excerpts and findings

### One-story / height restriction - pp. 3-4
> "and shall be limited to one story." (p. 3)
- City ordinance sections: Sec. 9.72.004 (G); Sec 7.37.004 (I).
- HCD identifies city height caps of 12, 14, or 16 feet depending on roof pitch, compared with underlying standards of 28 feet and two stories. HCD says the city must remove the restriction or permit the underlying zoning height.
- Cited statute: `Gov. Code, § 66300, subd. (b)(1)(A).` (p. 4)

### 800-square-foot limit - p. 4
> "the Total Floor Area shall not exceed 800 square feet" (p. 4)
- City ordinance sections: Sec. 9.72.004 (I); Sec 7.37.004 (K).
- The ordinance also imposed a 1,600-square-foot combined limit for two units, and applied an 800-square-foot cap to units on urban lot splits. HCD says the limits violate the Housing Crisis Act by reducing residential development capacity and must be removed.
- Cited statute: `Government Code section 66300, subdivision (b)(1)(A)` (p. 4)

### One-bedroom limit - p. 4
> "1 bedroom." (p. 4)
- City ordinance sections: Sec. 9.72.004 (I); Sec 7.37.004 (K).
- HCD says restricting bedrooms may have discriminatory effects on protected households and reduce permitted intensity of land use. HCD directs the city to revise this section.
- Cited statutes: `Government Code section 65008, subdivisions (a)(1)(A) and (b)(1)(B)(i)` and `Government Code section 66300, subdivision (b)(1)(A)` (p. 4).

### Other concerns - pp. 2-3
- Two-unit maximum on unsplit duplex lots: Sec 9.72.004 (C) and (E). HCD says the cap conflicts with ADU Law when there has been no lot split. Citation: `Gov. Code, § 65852.2, subds. (a) and (e).` (p. 2)
- Mandatory attachment of residential units: Sec 9.72.004 (C). HCD says detached ADUs must remain possible. Citations: `Government Code section 65852.2, subdivision (a)(1)(D)(iii)` and `subdivision (e)(1)(B)` (p. 3).
- Affordability deed restriction: Sec 9.72.004 (E); Sec. 9.72.004 (J)(10); Sec 7.37.004 (D). HCD raises feasibility concerns and recommends removal. Citations: `Government Code section 65850, subdivision (g)` and `section 65850.01` (p. 3).

### HCD conclusion - p. 5
- HCD concludes the ordinance's requirements reduce land-use intensity and raise issues under the HCA, ADU Law, Housing Element Law, Land Use Discrimination Law, and SB 9. HCD requests a written response within 30 days and a proposed corrective-action timeline.
- Enforcement citations: `Government Code section 65585, subdivision (j)`; `Gov. Code, § 65585, subd. (i).`; `Gov. Code, § 65585, subd. (j).` (p. 5)
- SB 9 footnote cites `Gov. Code, §§ 65852.21, 66411.7` (p. 5).
