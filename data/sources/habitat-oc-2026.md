## What they build and where

- Habitat OC builds affordable ownership homes for low- and moderate-income households, using staff, homeowners, and volunteers.
- Its Our Homes page lists single-family houses, attached duplexes, condominiums, and other homeownership opportunities.
- Listed locations include Irvine, Santa Ana, Fullerton, Rancho Santa Margarita, Cypress, Stanton, and Costa Mesa.
- Its Our Homes page states: "Habitat for Humanity of Orange County has built 239 homes in 17 cities over the past 35 years." (Our Homes, section "Orange County, CA")
- The same section states: "Our goal is to have 100 homes in our pipeline over the next three years." (Our Homes, section "Orange County, CA")
- Example: a planned two-story single-family home in Costa Mesa, approximately 1,226 square feet with two bedrooms and 1.5 bathrooms. (Our Homes, section "COMING SOON")

## Publicly stated permitting and approval experience

- The 2024 annual report says: "securing necessary approvals and permits in partnership with city council chambers and building departments." (2024 Annual Report, message from President and CEO)
- In the West Washington construction update, Habitat OC says: "After months of preparation, coordination, and patience, our building permit was officially approved on September 30." (Together We Build Progress in Santa Ana, opening paragraph; the update describes the 2025 project)
- These passages establish that permitting and coordination are meaningful parts of the development process. They do not establish that a particular city violated a statutory deadline or imposed an unlawful requirement.
- A 2026 Habitat OC article discusses funding reductions, financing gaps, and increasing construction costs as development obstacles. This is a financial constraint, not a documented permitting violation.
