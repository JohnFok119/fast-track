## What the City refused (p. 1)
- The applicant submitted an SB 9 urban lot split application on 2025-10-08.
- The property included a front single-family residence and a rear detached structure containing a 640 sf ADU and a 441 sf garage, constructed in 2020.
- The proposed split would place the ADU and garage on a new parcel, separate from the original primary residence.
- According to the HCD letter as transcribed, the City declined to accept and process the application because the proposed new parcel would have an ADU without a primary dwelling.
- The applicant proposed converting the ADU to a primary dwelling. The City indicated its code did not provide a process for that conversion.
- The letter does not specify the dates of either refusal.

## HCD findings (pp. 1-2)
- Existing ADUs or JADUs do not, by themselves, make an urban lot split ineligible under SB 9.
- The proposed ADU-to-primary-dwelling conversion can be consistent with SB 9 if all local and statutory requirements are met.
- The City must provide a regulatory pathway for the conversion to allow processing of the urban lot split application.
- Objective standards may apply when authorized by law; any denial must meet the statutory public-health-and-safety findings requirements.
- HCD requested a written response by 2026-06-05.
